## StackChan AI Inspector — Physical AI with Gemma 4

**Simple, Slim, Compact + Smart**

A Physical AI proof of concept using a locally hosted Gemma 4 model.

StackChan identifies the position of a toy vehicle, adjusts its camera direction through servo control, captures another image, and performs visual inspection.

The same Gemma 4 VLM is used for both object localization and appearance inspection by switching prompts.

**Verified on real hardware:**
- Orange SUV with white roof: OK
- Black pickup truck: NG
- Two-stage image capture, position-based servo commands, and Japanese speech reporting
